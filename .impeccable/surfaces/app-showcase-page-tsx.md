## Scope

Experience: five existing creative studies, including a linked image project and four live previews with source-code controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Give each working study enough room to experience the interface, with open image-and-caption framing and direct implementation access.

OWN-WORLD: Swiss-inspired product gallery with Geist Sans, neutral light and dark surfaces, blue actions in both themes, generous whitespace, and original-color imagery. IBM Plex Mono serves dates and technical metadata.

REFERENCE: Use the open, separated image-and-caption rhythm visible in [Helena Zhang's homepage](https://www.helenazhang.com/) while preserving the showcase's interactive study controls.

STORY: Visitors view the first study, browse the remaining gallery, switch between Preview and Code, and inspect available source tabs or external destinations.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact heading and existing introduction lead into a full-width first study. Remaining studies use two columns on desktop and one on mobile.

FORM: User-approved open gallery with image or iframe, title, concise caption, and direct controls; preserved iframe behavior, Preview/Code state, HTML/CSS/JS tabs, on-demand source fetching, and loading/error states. No random seed applies.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Contracts

Preserve source URLs, dates, content, iframe dimensions and lazy loading, safe external links, keyboard controls, theme persistence, and static export.
