# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is engineering leaders, recruiters, and potential collaborators evaluating Muhammad Irfan Fakhri as a software engineer. They need to understand his range, engineering judgment, and ability to ship dependable products quickly from the work itself.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Muhammad's selected software work, professional experience, and credentials. It succeeds when a visitor can quickly form a credible picture of how he works, find evidence in real projects, and confidently continue to his project details or professional profiles.

## Positioning

Muhammad works across product systems and interface craft: he connects dependable full-stack engineering, thoughtful interaction design, and real-world delivery rather than presenting either code or visual polish in isolation.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive through a job application, referral, professional profile, or shared project link. They scan first, then inspect selected work, experience, credentials, source repositories, and professional contact paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a statically exported Next.js portfolio and must remain fully functional without a Node.js production server.
- Existing project data, professional history, credentials, dates, links, analytics, and theme behavior are factual contracts.
- The homepage leads to selected work while `/projects`, `/showcase`, and `/log` remain distinct destinations.

## Brand Commitments

- Use Muhammad Irfan Fakhri and Katsuotz as the established identity.
- Keep the voice direct, technically credible, human, and free of unsupported claims.
- Preserve system-aware light and dark themes and the existing K/O favicon behavior.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project screenshots and project records in `public/project/` and the existing project components.
- Professional history and company marks in the existing work-experience components and `public/work/`.
- Education, certification, and achievement records in the existing credentials components and assets.
- Public GitHub and LinkedIn profiles linked from the current homepage.
- No testimonials, client logos, performance benchmarks, or commercial claims are available and they must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let shipped work provide the proof.
- Make engineering judgment legible before asking for attention.
- Reward both a fast scan and a deeper technical read.
- Preserve factual trust over promotional language.
- Keep every interaction accessible, responsive, and lightweight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain keyboard accessible, maintain visible focus and WCAG AA text contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences across light and dark themes.
